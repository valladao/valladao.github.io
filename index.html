This ensures clean, maintainable, and conflict-free styles while keeping performance and accessibility as top priorities.

**In summary:** my JavaScript experience spans over a decade—from ERP full-stack systems to headless Shopify storefronts. Today, I specialize in **React, TypeScript, Hydrogen/Remix**, and modern styling approaches such as **CSS Modules** and **Tailwind CSS**, delivering accessible, scalable, and high-performance frontends aligned with Shopify’s modern stack.
